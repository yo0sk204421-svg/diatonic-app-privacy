1. 収集する情報

本アプリは、利用者に関するいかなる個人情報も収集しません。本アプリはすべての機能を端末内（オフライン）で処理しており、サーバーへの通信、アカウント登録、広告表示、利用状況の解析（アナリティクス）などは一切行っていません。

本アプリは音声再生・音声合成機能（expo-audio, expo-speech）を利用しますが、これらはすべて端末内で完結する処理であり、外部への音声データの送信は行いません。

本アプリは、利用者の情報を第三者に提供、共有、販売することはありません。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

"Diatonic Trainer" (the "App") is a music theory learning app provided by the developer. This policy explains how user information is handled within the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information from users. All functionality runs entirely on-device (offline). The App does not communicate with any server, require account registration, display advertisements, or use analytics tools.

2. Device Permissions

The App uses audio playback and speech synthesis features (expo-audio, expo-speech), both of which are processed entirely on-device. No audio data is transmitted externally.

3. Third-Party Sharing

The App does not share, disclose, or sell any user information to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal information, it may be used by users of any age.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated without prior notice due to legal changes or the addition of new App features. Any changes take effect once posted on this page.
